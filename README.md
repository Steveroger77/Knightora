## ✨ What is Knightora?

Writing a feature-length screenplay or an epic fantasy novel is hard. As your story grows to dozens of scenes and hundreds of pages, keeping track of every character's traits, plot points, props, and timelines becomes overwhelming.

In professional film production, a whole team helps the writer:
- A **Script Supervisor** to watch for continuity errors.
- A **Production Designer** to visualize scenes and costumes.
- A **Dramaturg / Story Editor** to analyze pacing and dialogue punch.
- An **Archivist** to maintain the "Series Bible" of lore.

**Knightora brings that entire film crew directly to your browser.** Whether you are writing a 5-minute short film or a multi-season cinematic franchise, Knightora keeps your story organized, visually stunning, and logically airtight.

## 🌟 Key Features in Simple Words

### 1. Dual-Mode Editor (Screenplay & Novel)
- **Screenplay Mode**: Formats your writing automatically using standard Hollywood layout (Courier Prime font, scene headings/sluglines like `INT. COFFEE SHOP - DAY`, character cues, parentheticals, and dialogue blocks).
- **Novel / Manuscript Mode**: Switches with one click into elegant, prose-friendly typography for novels, short stories, and treatments.
- **Smart Formatting**: Hitting `Enter` or `Tab` automatically predicts what comes next (e.g. typing a Character name and pressing Enter automatically switches to Dialogue).

### 2. The Quill AI Director
- Highlight any line of text or dialogue in your script to summon **The Quill** context menu.
- **Instant Actions**:
  - *Punch Up Dialogue*: Add sharpness, wit, tension, or subtext.
  - *Sensory Expansion*: Add sights, sounds, smells, and atmospheric textures to action lines.
  - *Tone Shift*: Make a scene darker, more comedic, melancholic, or urgent.
  - *Tighten & Trim*: Cut unnecessary fluff to make scenes fast-paced.
- **Side-by-Side Diff Comparison**: See the AI suggestion side-by-side with your original text before you accept or reject it.

### 3. Automated Story Bible & Character Dossiers
- Keeps a living encyclopedia of your entire fictional world:
  - **Characters**: Track real names, aliases, physical traits, personality flaws, psychological wounds, and character arcs.
  - **Locations**: Document fictional cities, interiors, historical eras, and sensory atmospheres.
  - **Props & Artifacts**: Track important objects, MacGuffins, keys, and weapons.
  - **Factions & Houses**: Organize kingdoms, corporations, crime syndicates, and family trees.
  - **Scene Index**: Browse every scene beat in chronological order.

### 4. The Corkboard Relationship Graph
- An interactive visual node graph connecting characters, factions, and items.
- See alliances, rivalries, family lineages, and secret grudges at a glance with color-coded relationship lines.
- Drag and zoom around the canvas just like a real detective's corkboard in a writers' room.

### 5. Film Reel Timeline Scrubber
- A visual film-strip scrubber that displays your story's 3-Act Structure or multi-beat framework.
- Scrub horizontally across scenes to review story momentum, rising tension, the midpoint climax, and the final resolution.
- Click any frame on the reel to jump directly to that scene in the editor.

### 6. The Production Designer Image Studio
- Turn scene descriptions and character dossiers into high-resolution cinematic concept art powered by **FLUX.1**.
- **Cinematographer Camera Controls**:
  - *Lens selection*: 35mm, 50mm anamorphic, 85mm portrait, wide-angle 24mm.
  - *Lighting presets*: Golden hour, neon noir, dramatic chiaroscuro, overcast daylight, candlelit shadows.
  - *Aspect Ratios*: 16:9 cinematic widescreen, 2.39:1 anamorphic cinemascope, 4:3 vintage, 1:1 square.

### 7. Continuity Monitor (Plot Hole Detector)
- Knightora scans your scenes in the background to catch continuity mistakes before they turn into plot holes:
  - *Did a character enter a room they were never seen walking into?*
  - *Did an item appear in Scene 10 after being destroyed in Scene 3?*
  - *Did a character's eye color or backstory suddenly change?*
- Click any flagged issue to jump directly to the exact paragraph and resolve the contradiction.

### 8. The Oracle (Universe Lore Search)
- Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` from anywhere in the app to open **The Oracle**.
- Ask questions about your story in plain English:
  - *"Where did John hide the encrypted drive in Act 1?"*
  - *"Who was in the vault during the blackout?"*
  - *"What are the weaknesses of the iron guard?"*
- Knightora answers with **clickable citations** that take you straight to the scene or character dossier where that fact was established.

### 9. Script Reader Diagnostics & Brainstorming
- **AI Story Diagnostics**: Get an objective breakdown of your script’s pacing, emotional temperature, dialogue-to-action ratio, and scene velocity.
- **Brainstorming Engine**: Stuck at a plot fork? Ask the AI for 5 unexpected plot twists, high-concept catalysts, or high-stakes complications to break writer's block.

### 10. Scratchpad & Director Notes
- Built-in scratchpad right beside your script.
- Jot down raw dialogue snippets, brainstorm future scene ideas, or save links without cluttering your main manuscript.

### 11. Industry-Standard Import & Export
- **Import**: Easily import existing scripts in `.fountain`, `.txt`, `.md`, or `.json` formats.
- **Export**: Export clean, industry-compliant files:
  - **Fountain** (compatible with Final Draft, Highland, and Fade In)
  - **PDF** (formatted with standard 12pt Courier screenplay margins)
  - **Markdown**
  - **Microsoft Word (.docx)**

### 12. Split View & Sanctuary (Distraction-Free Mode)
- **Split Screen**: View your script on the left while simultaneously viewing your Concept Art, Story Bible, or Timeline on the right.
- **Sanctuary Mode (`Cmd/Ctrl + \`)**: Fades away every panel, button, and header so only your glowing cursor and your words remain. Press `Esc` to return.

### 13. Offline-First Architecture & Cloud Sync
- **Works 100% Offline**: No Supabase account required for local writing! All projects, character bibles, and settings are automatically saved safely in your browser's local database.
- **Optional Cloud Sync**: Connect your own free Supabase database whenever you want automatic cloud backups and team access across devices.

## 🚀 Quickstart & Installation

### Prerequisites
Make sure you have [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (version 18 or newer) installed on your computer.

### 1. Clone the Repository
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Steveroger77/Knightora.git
cd Knightora
```

### 2. Install Dependencies
```bash
cd frontend
npm install
```

### 3. Launch the Studio
```bash
npm run dev
```

Open your browser and navigate to **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**.  
*That's it! Knightora will open immediately with a complete demo story ready to explore.*

## 💡 How to Use Knightora: Step-by-Step Guide

Here is a simple walk-through of a typical writing session:

### Step 1: Create or Open a Project
- Click **Projects** in the top-left navigation or press `Cmd/Ctrl + N`.
- Enter a title, logline, genre (Sci-Fi, Noir, Fantasy, Thriller), and choose whether you want to write a **Screenplay** or a **Novel**.
- Alternatively, press `Cmd/Ctrl + I` to import an existing `.fountain` or text script.

### Step 2: Write Your First Scene
- In **Script Mode**, write your scene header (e.g. `EXT. OLD OBSERVATORY - NIGHT`).
- Press `Enter` to write action lines describing the environment.
- Press `Tab` to center a character name (e.g. `KARA`), then press `Enter` to type her dialogue.

### Step 3: Polish Dialogue with "The Quill"
- Highlight Kara's dialogue with your mouse.
- The floating **Quill** menu will appear above the text.
- Click **"Sharpen Subtext"** or **"Add Sensory Detail"**.
- Review the AI preview. Click **Accept** to replace or **Reject** to keep your original words.

### Step 4: Expand Your World in the Story Bible
- Click the **Story Bible** icon on the left rail (or switch to the Bible tab).
- Click **+ Add Character** to add a hero, mentor, or antagonist with their goals, fears, and appearance.
- Add key locations and props that will appear in multiple scenes.

### Step 5: Check Character Connections in the Corkboard
- Click the **Relationships** tab on the left rail.
- See your characters as interactive nodes on an illuminated corkboard.
- Connect characters to define friendships, alliances, rivalries, or secrets.

### Step 6: Generate Concept Art in the Image Studio
- Click the **Image Studio** tab on the left rail.
- Type a visual prompt or click **"Use Current Scene"**.
- Select your camera lens (e.g., *50mm Anamorphic*) and lighting (e.g., *Neon Noir*).
- Click **Generate** to create a cinematic visual frame for your mood board.

### Step 7: Ask The Oracle Questions
- Press `Cmd/Ctrl + K` anytime.
- Type: *"What weapon did Kara carry in the opening scene?"*
- The Oracle immediately searches your story bible and returns the answer with a link to that exact scene.

### Step 8: Export Your Masterpiece
- When you are ready to share your script, click **Export** in the top bar or press `Cmd/Ctrl + E`.
- Choose **PDF** for standard Hollywood submission format, **Fountain** for other screenplay editors, or **Markdown** for publishing.

## ⌨️ Director's Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Cmd/Ctrl + \`** | Toggle **Sanctuary Mode** (Distraction-free full-screen writing) |
| **`Esc`** | Exit Sanctuary mode / Close any active modal or split view |
| **`Cmd/Ctrl + K`** | Open **The Oracle** (Universe lore search & citation lookup) |
| **`Cmd/Ctrl + N`** | Create a **New Project** |

| **`Cmd/Ctrl + /`** or **`?`** | Open the **Keyboard Shortcuts Directory** |
| **`Highlight Text`** | Summon **The Quill** contextual AI director menu |

```
Knightora/
├── frontend/                     # React 18 + Vite Web Application
│   ├── src/
│   │   ├── components/
│   │   │   ├── analysis/         # Script Reader diagnostics & pacing drawers
│   │   │   ├── auth/             # Login, profile & Google authentication
│   │   │   ├── chrome/           # Header, shortcuts, project modals
│   │   │   ├── editor/           # TipTap ProseMirror screenplay/novel engine
│   │   │   ├── export/           # PDF, Fountain, DOCX export dialogs
│   │   │   ├── image-studio/     # FLUX.1 concept visualizer with camera controls
│   │   │   ├── layout/           # Three-panel responsive rail & split views
│   │   │   ├── relationship-graph/# Interactive corkboard node network
│   │   │   ├── search/           # The Oracle Cmd+K universe search modal
│   │   │   ├── story-bible/      # Characters, locations, props, and scene bibles
│   │   │   └── timeline/         # Film-reel sequential timeline scrubber
│   │   ├── lib/                  # Supabase client, offline detectors & helpers
│   │   ├── stores/               # Zustand state stores (Editor, UI, Lore, Auth)
│   │   └── index.css             # Obsidian & Violet Aurora design system
│   └── package.json
│
└── supabase/                     # Supabase Backend & AI Edge Functions
    ├── functions/                # Deno edge functions
    │   ├── ai-analyze/           # Script pacing and tone diagnostic
    │   ├── ai-brainstorm/        # Creative catalyst & twist generator
    │   ├── ai-text-action/       # Contextual Quill rewrite & dialogue polisher
    │   ├── consistency-check/    # Logic & continuity conflict auditor
    │   ├── generate-image/       # FLUX.1 scene concept art generator
    │   ├── story-bible-extract/  # Autonomous character/prop entity extractor
    │   └── story-search/         # Vectorized semantic lore search with citations
    ├── migrations/               # PostgreSQL schema & Row-Level Security
    └── seed.sql                  # Seed data for demo universe projects
```

### Core Technologies
- **Frontend Framework**: [React 18](https://react.dev/) + [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Editor Engine**: [TipTap](https://tiptap.dev/) / [ProseMirror](https://prosemirror.net/) with custom Hollywood screenplay formatting
- **Styling**: [TailwindCSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with custom **Violet Aurora Glassmorphism** palette
- **State Management**: [Zustand](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand) (low-latency, zero-overhead reactive state)
- **Animations**: [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
- **Icons**: [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Backend & Database**: [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL + Auth + Edge Functions)
- **AI Models Supported**: OpenRouter (Claude, Gemini 2.5 Flash, GPT-4o) & Cloudflare Workers AI (FLUX.1 Schnell)

## 🔧 Configuration & Environment Variables

Knightora works out of the box with zero external configuration required for writing and local storage.

If you wish to enable AI features and cloud sync, create a `.env` file in the `frontend/` folder:

```bash
cp frontend/.env.example frontend/.env
```

Set the following variables in `frontend/.env`:

```env
# Optional: Supabase configuration (for cloud sync across devices)
VITE_SUPABASE_URL=https://your-project.supabase.co
VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY=your-anon-key

# Optional: AI Provider (for The Quill, The Oracle, and Diagnostics)
VITE_OPENROUTER_API_KEY=your-openrouter-api-key
VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY=your-gemini-api-key
```

> **Note on Offline Mode**: If `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` is omitted, blank, or unreachable, Knightora automatically detects this and switches seamlessly to **Offline Local Storage Mode** without throwing any network errors.
